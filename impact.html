International

India outreach

We traveled to a region of India where robotics and engineering education was virtually absent. Across five classes at Madinaguda Government School in Telangana, we presented what robotics is and what careers it opens — overcoming a complete language barrier to do it. We let students drive a robot we'd built from real FTC parts, then ran a paper-slider engineering competition they went all out on.

Result: the school launched an engineering class, giving 400 students a path into STEM.

The team explained everything in a simple, easy to understand way, which made learning exciting and fun. I now dream of becoming an engineer.
— Sindhu, 7th grade, Madinaguda Government School (translated)
Local

Redmond Elementary Science Fair

We ran a booth showing kids what FIRST Robotics offers. We talked with about 50 kids, around 30 said they wanted to learn more, and we ran out of FIRST pamphlets.

Local

Redmond Library workshop

An intro-to-FIRST session capped with a hands-on engineering challenge. About 20 kids attended and 15 left interested in starting with FIRST.

Service

Meadow Park cleanup

In partnership with the Green Redmond Partnership we cleared bushes and weeds to make room for native shrubs, in a community we all call home.

FTC community

Lifting other teams

  • 23404 Bot Not Found — practice scrimmages to sharpen our driving, plus parts swaps whenever either of us was short.
  • 32954 Bot Got Found — helped start this rookie team and gave them a competitive environment to test in.
  • 30624 Pangolins in Disguise — practical guidance on planning and running outreaches.
  • 12611 TechNova — Coach Terry shared season planning advice and their early-season shooter with us.
  • FTC WA Discord — handed out cards to new teams so they'd find the same help we did. We've assisted 45+ teams there.
Industry

Learning from engineers

  • Meta Reality Labs — Vijay showed us how SLAM computer vision works in Project Aria. It's why our robot has custom navigation paths.
  • Microsoft — Dilip, who heads a cybersecurity branch, walked us through AI agents, LLMs, agent workflows, and RAG.
  • Amazon fulfillment center — a tour of robots transporting, packaging, and sorting at scale. We came back with ideas for our transfer.
  • Microsoft Garage — Sriram gave us hands-on time with the laser cutter, which we still use for robot parts.
