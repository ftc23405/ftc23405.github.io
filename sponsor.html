What your support does

It pays for robot parts, competition registration, and the outreach programs that reached more than 10,000 people last season — including the school in India that launched an engineering class for 400 students because we showed up.

In return you get your logo on our robot, our team apparel, our engineering portfolio, and this site — plus a team that will show up and talk about your work.

Where the money goes

We estimate a season budget up front from last year's spend, taxes, and the small necessities, then track every purchase in a shared sheet. Before we buy, we review the list again to decide whether each part is genuinely necessary.

  • Outreach25%
  • Additional parts22%
  • FIRST Washington10%
  • Tax9%
  • Unanticipated6%
  • Gamefield set4%
  • Everything else24%
